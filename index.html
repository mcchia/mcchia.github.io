How I Work

From problem to measured change.

The same sequence applies whether the starting point is a pattern in support tickets, a slow delivery process, or a new capability that has to work inside day-to-day operations.

  1. 01 Problem State the customer, user, or operational problem in their terms — before anyone picks a solution.
  2. 02 Constraints Clarify what is fixed — timelines, systems, policy, risk — and what is only assumed.
  3. 03 Diagnosis Look for patterns in tickets, feedback, QA findings, and process data to find the cause, not the symptom.
  4. 04 Approach Turn the diagnosis into requirements, a redesigned workflow, or an operating change, with clear acceptance criteria.
  5. 05 Stakeholders Align product, engineering, QA, operations, and support on scope, ownership, and trade-offs.
  6. 06 Execution Coordinate delivery through QA, UAT, release, documentation, and handover to the people who run it.
  7. 07 Measurement Check whether it worked — lead time, throughput, adoption, accuracy, or fewer repeat issues.
Working principles
Evidence before opinion
Structure before speed
Ask the right question first
A change counts once people use it
